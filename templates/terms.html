Legal & Community Standards

Terms of Service & Copyright

Transparent, fair rules designed to protect artists and ensure constructive feedback.

1. Copyright & Intellectual Property (100% Artist Reserved)

At Articrare, we believe that artists own their art. When you upload a sketch, drawing, or study to Articrare:

  • You retain 100% ownership and copyright of all your artwork files and intellectual property.
  • Articrare does not claim ownership, licensing rights, or commercial reproduction rights over your work.
  • You grant Articrare only a limited, non-exclusive license to host, display, and thumbnail your image on the platform so your peers can view and critique it.
  • You can delete your sketches or your account at any time.

2. Gemini AI Vision Analysis Policy

Articrare uses the Google Gemini Vision API to provide real-time automated visual analysis on line work, anatomy, contrast, and shading.

  • Your sketch images are transmitted solely for the purpose of generating your personal critique suggestions and score.
  • Your artwork is never sold, licensed, or used to train third-party public AI generative models.

3. Community Standards & Critique Etiquette

Articrare is dedicated to constructive, respectful artistic growth. By participating, you agree to:

  • Provide constructive, actionable feedback (e.g. noting specific lighting, anatomy, or line weight observations).
  • Zero tolerance for harassment, hate speech, bullying, or abusive comments.
  • Only upload sketches you created yourself or have full permission to share for educational study.

4. Privacy & Data Protection

We respect your digital privacy. We only collect the minimal information needed to operate your account (username, email, password hash). We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with any advertising brokers.
