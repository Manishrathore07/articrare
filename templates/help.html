Knowledge Base & FAQ

Help Center

Everything you need to know about sharing sketches, 3D inspection, and AI vision critique.

✦ How does Articrare's AI Vision Critique work?

When you upload a sketch and select a critique focus (e.g. Anatomy & Proportions, Shading & Contrast, or Linework), Articrare sends your drawing to the Google Gemini Vision model. The AI evaluates the visual composition, lighting values, and anatomy of your sketch using a structured artistic critique prompt, producing an objective rating (1–10), highlighting specific visual strengths, and offering targeted recommendations for improvement.

✦ How does the 3D Lightbox & Card Tilt work?

Our cards are built using a native 3D perspective engine. As you move your mouse across any sketch card, it tilts dynamically in 3D space with a moving specular spotlight. Clicking on any sketch opens the 3D Sketch Inspector where moving your cursor simulates natural paper lighting tilt to check graphite sheen and line contrast.

✦ How are Community Average Scores calculated?

Every registered artist can leave a 1–10 star rating alongside a written critique. Articrare aggregates all community reviews and computes a live, real-time average score displayed prominently on each artwork card.

✦ Who owns the copyright to my uploaded artwork?

You do. 100%. You retain full copyright and ownership of every drawing, study, and sketch you share on Articrare. We never sell, distribute, or license your images without your consent.

✦ How do I get the most useful critique on my sketches?

Be specific in your Artist Notes! Mention what tools you used (e.g. 2B pencil, dip pen, digital brush) and specifically where you felt stuck (e.g. "struggled with foreshortening on the left arm" or "unsure if cast shadows are deep enough"). Peers and Gemini AI will focus their feedback directly on those areas.
